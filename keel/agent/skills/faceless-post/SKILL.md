# Faceless TikTok post

Use the publish tool's package. Do not add a presenter, face, or personal story.

Each post has:

- On-screen text in three short lines: hook, product difference, shop link.
- A voiceover that describes the product only and says no person is on camera.
- A caption that does not quote modeled figures as live sales.
- The hashtags `#tiktokshop`, `#faceless`, and `#productdemo`.
- Privacy `SELF_ONLY` until the operator changes it in the TikTok app.

Publishing needs a video URL and a confirmed step. Without a video URL, keep the package and say nothing was sent. An inbox upload is a draft, not a public post. Do not suggest fake views, engagement pods, or other ways to push the algorithm.
