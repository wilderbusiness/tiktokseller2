# Ranking rubric

Load the catalog with the ranking tool before you explain a score. Use the tool output. Do not recompute a different score in prose.

Competitiveness, from 0 to 100, mixes three parts:

- Half is the price gap: retail minus landed cost, divided by retail.
- Thirty percent is review density. A few hundred reviews score higher than none or than a saturated listing.
- Twenty percent is the offer. A specific product difference scores higher than a blank offer.

Market share is product units over category units for 7 days. If either number is missing, the share is empty. Do not estimate it.

Unit economics:

- Landed cost is the cheaper of the Alibaba and Temu unit costs, plus shipping.
- Shopify fee is 2.9% of retail plus $0.30.
- Contribution margin is retail minus landed cost minus that fee.
- Break-even ROAS is retail divided by contribution margin, only when margin is positive. Call it a model, not a live Meta result.

The overall score averages the parts that could be calculated. Rank higher scores first. Keep each figure's source and date. Modeled inputs dated 21 Sep 2026 stay modeled in every derived figure.
